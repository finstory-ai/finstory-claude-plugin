# Topic lenses for finstory story pages

Each page is shown with a topic, passed to the finstory connector's `story_get_display_pack` tool. The topic picks the lens you read the page through: a CFO's framing, not a checklist to work down. Pick the topic that matches what the page is about.

| Topic | Lens |
| --- | --- |
| P&L | Revenue quality, the margin stack, operating-cost leverage and the earnings trajectory. Separate mix from volume, and one-offs from run-rate. |
| Cash | Cash conversion, runway against obligations, seasonality, and bridge-style thinking across working capital, capital expenditure and financing. |
| Capital | Balance-sheet strength, leverage, equity thickness, capacity to service debt, and discipline with invested capital. |
| Working Capital | Receivables, payables and inventory, with days-sales, days-payables and days-inventory style reasoning. Quote exact ratios only when the data contains them. |
| KPI | A small set of named KPIs, their variance against target or prior period, and whether the page tells a control story or a growth story. |
| Other | General CFO framing: materiality, trend against point-in-time, risk against opportunity, and what a board would challenge first. |

## Using a lens

- Let the lens decide what you lead with and which two to four numbers become the page's questions.
- A lens is a way of reading the figures in the pack, not a list of things to cover. If the page doesn't show something the lens mentions, leave it out rather than estimating it.
